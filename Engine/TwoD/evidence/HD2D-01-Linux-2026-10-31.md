## What was tested

Full C/Zig sizes, alignment and field offsets; all complete original-through-new services prefixes
with byte canaries; unsupported old/absent hosts; atomic copies and retained immutable snapshots;
accepted vs active revisions; failed batches preserving pending/active state; owner-thread and
version failures; stale identity/mask rejection; polygon crossing/repeated vertex/backtracking;
valid concave polygons; 32 lights/8 shadowed capacity and duplicate rejection; exact 2048-edge
acceptance and excess rejection. ABI mutation tests reject C/Zig reordering, absent baselines and
missing callback metadata.

without changing the intersection rule. Initial configure attempts exposed missing local SDK
search paths; existing headers/libraries were supplied without changing repository build policy.
The first complete CTest had 289 cases, 4 failures and 13 skips because the new getter lacked
API-M6 metadata. Added an independent effects baseline, callback metadata and mutation guards;
the complete configure/build/CTest was rerun successfully as above. No assertion was removed.
Raw first/final logs and JUnit files remain in the task's external evidence directory.
