# HD2D-04: planar GPU angular soft shadows

Status: IN_PROGRESS, not accepted. Beads `nexora-m6m.4`; engineering remains 2/7 until
HD2D-03 and this stage pass applicable current-head CI. Physical PC: NOT_RUN.

## Implemented boundary

Native compute on DX12/Vulkan/Metal writes 1,024 nearest-distance samples for each of at most
eight shadow lights. Up to 256 explicitly supplied simple contours, 16 vertices each and 2,048
total edges are validated before conservative culling. No alpha-derived contours or CPU shadow
texture fallback. A 9-tap tent with wrap interpolation uses at most 18 reads per receiver/light;
bias is explicit world distance. Ambient/HUD stay independent. Existing Sprite and C/Zig wires
are unchanged. Completion-protected per-frame buffers retire after fences; diagnostics separate
shadow allocation bytes from scene target bytes and do not claim resident VRAM or GPU duration.
